Habit tracker widgets for iPhone: keep your habits in sight

Updated October 2026 · 4 minute read

The short answer: a habit you see every time you pick up your phone is a habit you remember. Habit Pups has Home Screen widgets in three sizes and a round Lock Screen widget whose ring fills as you finish the day's habits. Adding one takes under a minute.

Why widgets help habits stick

Habits run on cues. The most reliable cue is one you can't avoid, and few things are seen more often than a phone's Home Screen and Lock Screen. A widget turns your habits from something you have to remember to check into something that's simply there: a pup still waiting, or a ring that's nearly full.

The Habit Pups widgets

  • Small: one of your dogs, a different one each time you look.
  • Lock Screen: the pup of your choice in a ring with one segment per habit due today. Each habit you finish lights up a segment, and the fuller the ring, the happier the face.

The dogs in the widgets show how each habit is going: happy once it's done, waiting while it isn't, and a little sad after a missed day. The yard follows the seasons too, with sunflowers in summer and snow in winter.

How to add a Home Screen widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Habit Pups.
  4. Swipe to the size you want and tap Add Widget, then Done.

How to add the Lock Screen widget

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen, then tap Customize and choose Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the space under the clock.
  3. Pick Habit Pups, then tap the widget to choose which dog it shows.
  4. Tap Done.

Which one should I use?

If you have one or two habits, the Lock Screen ring is the most effective: you see it every time you check the time. With more habits, add the large widget to your first Home Screen page so the whole kennel is in view.
