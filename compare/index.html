Habit Pups vs Finch, Habitica and Streaks

Updated October 2026

The short answer: Habit Pups is a habit tracker for iPhone where each habit is a puppy that celebrates, learns tricks and misses you. Pick it if you want something warm and playful that stays in view through widgets. Finch is a self-care pet app, Habitica turns habits into a role-playing game, and Streaks is a clean, minimal tracker. All four are good; they suit different people.

Choose Habit Pups if…

  • You've tried checklist trackers and drifted away after a few weeks.
  • You want to see your habits on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, not just in an app.
  • You'd like a reason to keep going: 14 tricks and 9 rewards earned by streaks, from a high five at day 3 to a crown at a year.
  • You want habits that don't all have to be daily: every day, certain days, or a few times a week.
  • You care about privacy: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your data stays in your own iCloud.

Choose something else if…

  • You need Android today, or a web app (Finch and Habitica both have them; Habit Pups for Android is coming soon).
  • You want mood tracking and journaling prompts built in (Finch is built around them).
  • You want to team up with friends on quests (Habitica's parties do that).
  • You want the most minimal list possible, without characters (Streaks).
